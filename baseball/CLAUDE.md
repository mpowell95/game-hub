## The stats shape (`js/game-stats.js`)

`ensureBb(g)` builds `g.bb` with this exact key list, frozen, and the list `players-agg.js`'s merge branch must stay in step with (28 keys became 42 at the phase 0 amendment, 2026-09-12; `runs` renamed `runsScored`).

**Additive integer counters (default 0, this order):** `careersStarted`, `careersFinished`, `seasons`, `forfeits`, `wsTitles`, `perfectSeasons`, `hits`, `doubles`, `triples`, `homers`, `atBats`, `runsScored`, `walksDrawn`, `runsAllowed`, `hitsAllowed`, `walksIssued`, `inningsPitchedOuts`, `strikeoutsBatting`, `strikeoutsPitched`, `sacFlies`, `sacBunts`, `rbi`, `stolenBases`, `caughtStealing`, `pickoffs`, `homersAllowed`, `bronzes`, `silvers`, `golds`, `shutouts`, `walkoffWins`, `extraInningGames`, `noHitters`, `perfectGames`, `grandSlams`. **Math.max only (rule 2):** `bestRunsGame`, `bestStrikeoutsPitchedGame`, `bestWinStreak`. **Then:** `bestLeague` (highest rung 1-5), `bestTrophyByLeague` (per league, Math.max), `hand` (`null` or `{ v, at }`, written ONCE by `setBaseballHand`, never overwritten, warns on a later attempt, rejects non-`BB_HANDS`), `history` (object keyed by careerId, never pruned).

`atBats` = plate appearances minus `walksDrawn`, `sacFlies`, `sacBunts`; `runsScored` is the player's team; `seasons` increments when a season RESOLVES (after the championship game, or when the regular season ends with no playoff place); `forfeits` is a breakout of `total.lost`. **Derivable but stored, never recomputed, do not add a third copy:** `careersFinished` (= number of `history` rows), `bestLeague`, and `bronzes/silvers/golds` (trophies WON; `bestTrophyByLeague` loses repeats). Missed-playoff seasons = `seasons` minus their sum, not a field.

- `recordBaseball(league, won, extras)` is additive and idempotent BY CONTRACT ONLY: the caller calls it at most once per finished game; the writer does not de-duplicate. A failed write queues (`gamehub.pendingResults.v1`) and replays via `applyBaseball`. `bronzes/silvers/golds` are earned only via `extras.trophy`.
- `recordBaseballCareerFinished(row)` unions one `history` row by `careerId` (replaced only if the incoming `endedAt` is later), then bumps `careersFinished`. `recordBaseballCareerStarted()` bumps `careersStarted`. Both are lifecycle events with NO rate gate (`tools/test-rate-guard.mjs` `EXEMPT`).

## Engine rules in force (`baseball/js/engine/`)

- **Pure and seeded.** No `Math.random`, `Date.now`, DOM, storage or network in `engine/` (`test.js` section 2 reads every engine file as text). All randomness via `rng.js` (`stepRng`), passed as a parameter. `career.js` also has no clock/storage and returns a NEW state (argument byte-identical afterwards). Seeds derive via `hashSeed` from `careerId` + season number (both of a person's phones play identical games).
- **A pitch/swing cycle is the atomic unit a snapshot can interrupt between, never inside**; `abort()` is honoured only BETWEEN passes. Never `return` on `aborted` between a steal and the `count` emit (cost a red resume gate). Gate: `test.js` 12b's 24-seed x 40-stop resume sweep.
- **`SNAP_V` (2) / `RULES_V` (4) are forward-only, never reinterpreted** (doc section 15): a mismatched snapshot is REJECTED, not resumed with guessed fields. A bump costs at most one game's progress, never history.
- **Any behaviour change that would alter a season in progress is gated by a per-season snapshotted flag**; an older season/snapshot keeps its old rules to its last game. Existing on `season`: `livePlays`, `runControl`, `fieldControl`, `runSpeedV`, `wallHeight`, `parks`, `standingsModel`, `points`, `cap`, `schedule`, `games`, `slots`, `playoffFormat` (readers `seasonGames/seasonSlots/seasonPlayoffFormat` fall back to the pre-R16 shape: 12 games, eight slots, top-4 cut). Points are paid from the SEASON's snapshot of `POINTS`, never today's table.
- **Everything is decided in the ENGINE and emitted as an event; the UI only presents it** (`ui.js` asks `game._stealCandidate(side)`). Agents are pluggable; the engine legalizes the return (unknown pitch -> fastball, missing decision -> take).
- **CPU batters may never time or place better than a median human, at any league or slot** (doc section 8 [Locked]; `test.js` 17/19; `CPU_SIGMA_ABSOLUTE_FLOOR_MS` 58 vs human 55). `guess` only weights a location lean, never base placement.
- **Pitch/swing model.** `aim {x,y}`, strike = `|x|<=1 && |y|<=1`; a break is a fact of pitch TYPE + pitcher (`BREAK_OFFSET`, `pitchSpin`) applied at the plate, aimed at `target - break` (`ui.js`'s `_pitchBreakUnits` reads the human pitcher's real `pitchSpin`, so the cursor shows the true crossing). Speeds are travel-time multiples (`PITCH_TRAVEL_MULT`). The timing window scales by `timeToPlateS / referenceFlightS`; travel time divides by the LEAGUE's fastball readout (`READOUT` mph is display only). `LEAGUE_TIMING_WINDOW_MULT` (little 1.6 .. majors 0.8) scales BOTH `swing()` and `buntSwing()`; CPU `timingSigmaMs` is separate.
- **Contact (R5).** Placement steers the ball, it NEVER subtracts power; the rim costs launch-angle tightness only. Exit velocity: `PERFECT/BARELY_TIMED/CAP_POWER_EXIT_VELO_MPH` 80/50/105. **No ball in play ever carries 0 ft** (`GROUND_CARRY_FACTOR`, `MIN_IN_PLAY_FT` 40). Carry's angle factor is a half-sine peaking at `CARRY_PEAK_DEG` 30 (NOT `sin(2a)`). The fence check reads `kind === 'fly' || kind === 'line'`. `LEAGUE_POWER_SCALE`: a q=1 swing at `MEDIAN_HIT_POW_PTS` carries `MEDIAN_CARRY_FRAC` of the league's centre fence. `zones.js` depths are FEET and reach the wall on purpose, so the double/triple ladder only decides a ball hit into a GAP.
- **Home runs must clear the wall's HEIGHT** (8 ft base, `WALL_RULE`, tall sections in `PARKS`): below it is `wall-double` (`wall-triple` within 40 deg of a foul line), `distanceFt` pulled to the wall's foot. The drawn arc and the call share `battedApexFt`, so they cannot disagree.
- **Contact grid:** `--contact-grid`'s `ratio`/`cross`/`ceiling` assertions failed after R5 (only satisfiable when home runs never/always happen). **Do not weaken the thresholds; do not chase them with `exitVeloMphPerPt`.**
- **RA rules.** A pickoff consumes no pitch and leaves the count (`PICKOFF_MAX_PER_AT_BAT` 3). Steal: only first and second (never home); resolved after the swing is scored; **void if the batter puts the ball in play**; a caught steal's third out ends the half and the batter keeps the lineup pointer. A mistimed bunt is a foul, never a swinging miss; a foul bunt with two strikes is strike three. `sacrifice` is an out whose name does not end in "out": `outcomeWord`, `_settleAtBat`'s marker and `_animateRunners`'s `wasOut` must each know it (grep `/out$/`).
- **Bunt (batch 2).** Position, never timing: `buntSwing` reads the same cursor/pitch offset against a bar (`BUNT_BAR_HALF_X/Y`); outside the bar no contact, past `BUNT_FOUL_X_FRAC` foul, `offY` past `BUNT_POPUP_Y_FRAC` = **`bunt-popup`** (always an out, no roll). `game.js` sends `battedKind: 'bunt-popup'` (NOT the shared `'popup'`, whose apex floor is too high). CPU bunts read the identical check.
- **Live plays (4a/4b/5/6).** `liveplay.js` is pure, seeded, ONE synchronous call inside the pitch pass (a resume starts at the pitch boundary). **Reached in the air = caught = always an out.** A fielding bobble scores like any ball not caught: doc section 10 has no error outcome and `test.js` forbids an `'error'` string in the engine. **A later input never changes anything before it** (draws in time order): that is what lets the UI re-resolve the whole play on every tap (`test.js` 36-38). `view.resolve(...)` replays from a PRIVATE RNG copy; the agent's return is booked from the real RNG (booked play = last drawn play). No run scores on a play whose third out is a force or the batter before first. Arm = pitch Speed, aim = pitch Accuracy; wild past 10 ft = every runner takes a base. Live seasons use `LIVE_PLAY.cpuRosterLevel`, `hitPowMphPerPt` 0.4, per-league `carryMult` (all `settings.js`).
- **Season shape (decided, measured).** `BRACKET_MODEL = strongestInFinal`, `PLAYOFF_HOME = higherSeed`, `SCHEDULE_SHAPE = repeatMiddle` (confirmed by Matt), `STANDINGS_MODEL = withResults` (`scaledTo12` was measured catastrophic).
- **Ladder shape.** `TEAM_LADDER_OFFSETS` is GENERATED from `LADDER_SHAPE` (`cliff`/`spread`/`steep`), never hand-typed; `CHAMPION_CEILING` stops a champion out-pitching the next league. `STYLE_STRENGTH_DELTA` is STALE. Do not hand-pick an Aces/Sluggers slot to fix `CHAMPION_IS_HARDEST` (the sigma/chase axis is saturated; cross-slot order comes from `behaviorMul`/`cornerBias`/`pitchMix`/`changeupShare`).
- Also decided: a hit of N bases advances everyone N; a sac fly scores only the runner from third; no fielding stat; `effectiveCapFor(league)` = `CAPS - CPU_LEVEL_SHORTFALL` (CPU below the cap, never at it; effective caps non-decreasing by league, `test.js` section 8). Sim stand-ins `ModelRunner`/`ModelFielder`/`HUMAN_*`/`paint` exist because the sim player once could not see Speed or Accuracy.

# Contexto word data

`en.json`, `en.bin`, `es.json` and `es.bin` are GENERATED by `build-contexto-data.mjs` (repo root).
Do not edit them by hand; re-run the generator.

- **Word vectors:** fastText Common Crawl vectors `cc.en.300` and `cc.es.300`, by Facebook AI
  Research, https://fasttext.cc/docs/en/crawl-vectors.html, licensed **CC BY-SA 3.0**. The
  shipped files are a derived work: the most frequent words only, reduced to 64 principal
  components and quantized to 8 bits, plus each secret word's 1,000 nearest neighbours computed
  from the full vectors. They are shared under the same licence.
  Reference: E. Grave, P. Bojanowski, P. Gupta, A. Joulin, T. Mikolov, "Learning Word Vectors
  for 157 Languages", LREC 2018.
- **Inflected forms:** lemmatization-lists by Michal Boleslav Mechura,
  https://github.com/michmech/lemmatization-lists, licensed **ODbL**. Used to map a guess such
  as "cats" or "gatos" to its dictionary word.
- **Excluded words:** List of Dirty, Naughty, Obscene, and Otherwise Bad Words,
  https://github.com/LDNOOBW/List-of-Dirty-Naughty-Obscene-and-Otherwise-Bad-Words,
  licensed **CC BY 4.0**. Words on it are left out of the vocabulary (a short allow-list in the
  generator keeps ordinary words it over-catches, like "martillo").
- **Dictionaries used as filters:** ENABLE (public domain) and RLA-ES (MPL 1.1), the same files
  Boggle ships; see `boggle/data/CREDITS.md`.
- **Secret words:** chosen by hand, listed in `build-contexto-data.mjs`.

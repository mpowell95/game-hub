# Word list credits

`words.json`, `levels.json` and `daily.json` (en and es) are GENERATED by `tools/build-word-ladder-data.mjs` from:

- **Word Weaver's levels** (`word-weaver/data/`, see `word-weaver/data/CREDITS.md`: SCOWL, ENABLE, FrequencyWords).
- **ENABLE** (public domain) and **RLA-ES** (MPL 1.1), via `boggle/data/` (see `boggle/data/CREDITS.md`).
- **lemmatization-lists** Spanish, by Michal Boleslav Mechura, https://github.com/michmech/lemmatization-lists,
  licensed **ODbL 1.0**: Spanish verb forms and Ñ spellings (`tools/word-ladder-es-forms.txt`, a derived database
  shared under the same licence).
- **FrequencyWords** Spanish 2018 (OpenSubtitles), by Hermit Dave, https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords,
  licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**: which Spanish forms are said often enough to count.

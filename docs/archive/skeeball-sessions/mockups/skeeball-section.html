Why you only saw it sometimes

The green path is a firm throw. It peaks at 1.41 m, passes under the pane with 0.25 m to spare, and lands on the face. The red path is thrown harder. It climbs past 1.5 m, the top of the ball catches the ceiling at 1.58 m, and it comes straight back down the lane at you. Nothing on screen is there to hit — which is exactly why it read as an invisible object.

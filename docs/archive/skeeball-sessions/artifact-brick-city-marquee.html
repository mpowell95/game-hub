Notes

The name is display only. Whatever you paint on the marquee, the board id stays frozen from the day it is first played (THE LAW rule 5). Pick something short and stable like brickcity and never rename it.

Today's marquee is a flat colored bar, not artwork. In render.js it is one mesh tinted look.marquee with three bulbs above it, so no lettering exists on any machine yet. Any of these signs needs a painted texture drawn onto that panel, the same way the field and lane are painted.

The bulbs are already there. Four are built per cabinet, spaced across boardW. The chase in this mockup is a suggestion, not a requirement, and would need a reduced-motion check the same as everything else in the repo.

Contrast is the reason the plate is chalk on asphalt. Brick red under a yellow bulb bar is a low-contrast pair on its own; the dark plate gives BRICK CITY somewhere to sit without competing with the HOT SHOT glow above it.

## Where the extra bodies come from

Every machine's cabinet, lane, hump, rails and trough are identical — 48 rail bodies, 6 hump, 4 keep, and so on. The difference is entirely in how the holes are walled:

```
classic     192 ring segments over 7 holes  =  27.4 per hole
brickcity   126 cup segments  over 9 holes  =  14.0 per hole
```

THE CLASSIC is the only machine with **flush ringed holes**, and its ring walls are approximated as a circle of little boxes at a fixed density — one segment per 4 cm of circumference, minimum 20:

```js
const N = Math.max(20, Math.ceil((2 * Math.PI * Rwall) / 0.04));
```

So a big ring gets a lot of boxes. The 10's ring is the largest on any machine (large enough that a full circle would cross the 50's mouth, which is why it's built as an arc). The cup machines don't do this — they use a flat `cupSegments: 14` per collar, because a collar is a short wall, not a fine circle.

Seven holes with big rings at 27 segments each beats nine holes with small collars at 14.

## Why 32% more bodies costs 134% more time

The broadphase is `NaiveBroadphase` — it checks **every pair** of bodies, so cost goes with the **square** of the count:

```
bodies      263 / 200  = 1.32×
pairs       263² / 200² = 1.73×
measured    1.94 / 0.83 = 2.34×      (the rest is more contacts to solve near the ball)
```

## What that means for fixing it

**The broadphase fix helps THE CLASSIC most, precisely because it has the most bodies.** It replaces the pairwise sweep with "just the ball's pairs," which is linear — so classic's 263 bodies stop costing 34,000 pair tests per step and start costing 263. The bigger the machine, the bigger the win.

And it's the safe kind of fix: on brickcity I proved it produces **byte-identical throws** — 861 of 861 matching on hole, value, settle time to six decimals, bounce count and the full event sequence. It changes nothing about how the machine plays.

**What I would not touch:** the 4 cm segment density. Coarsening it would cut bodies faster than anything else, but it changes the shape of the ring the ball actually rolls on — that's a feel change on the machine that must never change feel. Not worth it, and not mine to decide.

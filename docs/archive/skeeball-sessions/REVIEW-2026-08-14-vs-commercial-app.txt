## Blockers — wrong information shown to the player

**How to play is factually false, on three of its five lines.** Measured against `tune-ladder.mjs` and `skeeball/js/test.js`:

| The sheet says | Measured |
|---|---|
| "Half strength = the 50 cup" | 0.50 power is the **40**. The 50 band starts at 0.62 |
| "The corner 100s take nearly full strength" | first 100 at **power 0.36** with full aim |
| "Too soft for the hump and the ball rolls back" | **cannot happen** — the softest step on the dial still scores 20 |

The diagram is wrong too: it shows only 20/30/40/50 (no 10, no 100s — half the board), and its "full strength" arc sails clean over everything, which no throw does. Fix `howto.js` + `strings.js` together.

**The hub blurb is wrong**: "Nine balls, five rings, two corner pockets worth 100." The board has four cups, one unlabelled painted ring, and a 10 trough.

## The board does not look like the machine in the footage

This is the biggest gap, and it's all in `render.js` → `_paintField` / `_buildMachine`:

- **Cups are flat black ovals with a hairline rim.** In the footage every ring is a raised white cylinder nested inside the next. Ours reads as spots painted on a plank.
- **The big painted ellipse is unlabelled and scores nothing.** In the real machine that ring *is* the 10. Ours puts "10" on the flat lip *below* the board, past a black gap.
- **Every value is printed twice, and none of them is on its own hole.** "100" prints below and outboard of its corner cup, directly under the "40" — a player reads the corner cups as 40s. In the footage each number appears once, on the ring face.
- **The 100s sit level with the 40.** In the footage they're tubes at the top corners, above the 50.
- **No backboard art.** The footage has the logo panel above the rings; ours is bare orange, then a black bar.
- **Black wedges either side of the board's bottom edge** read as holes in the geometry.
- **The board is ~16% of screen height** (the handoff's own number, against its 32–40% target). The footage gives the board ~45%. Two-thirds of our screen is empty lane with a very large ball on it.

So the three record rows render as a browser-default `<dl>` — value indented on its own line under the label — and both buttons render as unstyled UA buttons. `.sk-tiles` right below them *is* styled, which makes the mismatch obvious.

## HUD

- **TOP / BEST / TODAY never update.** They're written once when the HUD is built. The async top-score fetch only fills the *setup* screen's slots (`data-rec-top`), and `_paintHud()` only touches score and pips. So they show "-" through a first player's whole first rack, and stale values after "Roll again."
- In the hub, the floating "‹ Hub" pill lands right beside the HUD's ☰ — two leave-controls in the same corner.
- No ball number. The footage counts "BALL 9 → 1" in the marquee; we have nine 6px dots.
- The "Swipe up the lane to roll" hint sits on top of the ball.

## Gameplay — there is no downside

Straight-on, the entire dial scores 20/30/40/50. The 10 and 0 are unreachable without aiming into a corner:

- Random swiping averages ~36/ball ≈ **325 for a rack**.
- 22% of the dial (0.62–0.83) is one continuous 50 band, so "about 70%, straight" is 450 every time. My first-ever rack was **490**; the repo's own play probe scored **450**.
- The top of the dial is noise: 0.84→1.00 is 17 steps alternating 20/30/40/50/40/50/30/50. That's the failing assertion (`15 of 21 bands are one step wide`) — a hard swipe is a coin flip.

Per your standing ban this has to be fixed in geometry, not by steering the ball, so it needs your call on which way: make the soft end reach the 10/trough, or put a real price back on overshoot. It's the one item here that's a design decision rather than a defect.

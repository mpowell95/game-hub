## Where the numbers come from

- Read-only copies taken 2026-11-16, about 2:15 PM Eastern: the server-built leaderboard, and the play log.
- Real people only: 35 named people. Left out: test1 / DREG5, hidden test names, nameless devices, "Guest", and the
  admin TEST area. No simulation, computer-player setting, or "est" number is used.
- **Wins and games per level:** every game ever recorded, up to Nov 16 (no dates are stored on them).
- **Options (reracks, computers at the table, goals):** only games from Oct 30 on.
- **Game length:** the gap between one finish and that person's previous finish of the same game, under 30 minutes,
  Oct 28 on. It includes time on the setup screen.
- The yardstick is **points per game played** = points for a win x how often real people win. If a level pays more
  but is won less often in proportion, it comes out level.
- Win rates depend on who chose to play a level. Most Hard games are two people (King of Games and MattyIce).

Most of the tables hold up: at Medium and Hard, nearly every game pays between 0.9 and 1.4 points per game played.

## 2. Between subgroups

- **No subgroup is out of line with the others.** Where there is volume, strategy, luck, and aim games all pay about
  0.9 to 1.4 points per game played at Medium and Hard. Luck games are not paying more for less.
- **Per minute** the spread is wider, because games differ in length: Filler and Flick Soccer (under 40 seconds a
  game) pay about 2 a minute at Hard; Mancala and Cup Pong about 0.5 to 0.8. Points never mix across boards and a
  medal is a place on one board, so this does not make any board unfair.
- **Online** pays 1.0 to 1.5 per game played everywhere it has volume (Cup Pong 158 games, Hoops 136, Flick Soccer
  82, Escoba 75). That is in line with each game's own Hard level. Online win rates are always near 50%, so they
  cannot show how hard an online win is.
- **The 20-point cap:** the capped levels are the right ones (Tic Tac Toe Easy 91%, Flick Soccer Easy 98%, Cup Pong
  Medium 92%, Battleship Easy 11 of 11). One is missing: Air Hockey Medium. Five people have reached a cap, so 20 is
  doing its job; there is no data to say 15 or 30 would be better.
- **Quits** against the computer are recorded as losses, so they are already inside the win rates. 25 quit losses
  are logged since Oct 28 (17 Cup Pong, 5 Air Hockey).
